# Meta's Muse can get your money back #AI #meta #muse #agent

Number one, what is it actually like to hand a real life job that saves money to Muse and have it save me the time and the money that it would otherwise take to do that job? And then number two, why does doing that small personal job have one of the largest retailers in the world paying attention? But first, let's get into Muse. Look at the jobs people are already handing over. Chris Abraham said Muse found a book subscription his family had forgotten to cancel and canceled it, checked the refund policy, and recovered a year's worth of payments. He doesn't give a dollar figure for that, but it's going to be sizable. The interesting part is that Muse went ahead and pursued and went after that refund. How often do you cancel something and decide arguing about the old charges you didn't intend would take more effort than it was worth. Another early Muse user named Brad, these are all reports that I found around the internet, right? They're publicly available. He reports that Muse spent 98 minutes on the phone with AT&T through multiple transfers while also contacting Verizon and T-Mobile to compare phone offers. He says it spared him more than 3 hours of calls. Getting those hours back is something that you can't put a price on. Imagine multiplying that across 100 million people. For so many of us, the bills, the subscriptions, these problems have existed long before AI models came around, right? How long have you looked at paperwork and said, "I can't do all of this?" Or looked at charges and said, "I don't have time to investigate this." Or maybe a health bill you can't trust or haven't had time to get on the phone and call.
